# Example: Keyva

Ten reels made with Repo2Reels for [keyva.ae](https://keyva.ae), Dubai property research built on Dubai
Land Department records. Each reel takes one fact a buyer or tenant in Dubai is unlikely to know, and shows
where Keyva answers it.

| Reel | Hook |
|---|---|
| r11 | There's no JVC in Dubai's rent records. |
| r12 | Buying from India? You can send USD 250,000 a year. |
| r13 | Yes, Dubai has a knowledge fee. |
| r14 | Most price tools always give a number. Keyva sometimes won't. |
| r15 | Off-plan? Don't pay the developer directly. |
| r16 | Rent going up? Check the date on the notice. |
| r17 | That car loan could shrink your mortgage. |
| r18 | That 10% deposit? It's not a law. |
| r19 | No permit number? That's the red flag. |
| r20 | Dubai publishes what homes actually sell for. |

Make one: `python3 make.py projects/keyva r11` (about 6 minutes on two cores, the first time).

Everything here is a pattern to copy: `project.json` (brand, lexicon, lines, pauses, caption fixes, harmony),
`scenes.js` (hook, night card, decks, flips, receipts, timelines, counters, stamps, annotations) and
`scenes.css`. `share-copy.txt` is the caption that goes with each reel.

Figures and rules in the reels were correct for their stated dates. They are general information, not legal,
tax or financial advice.
