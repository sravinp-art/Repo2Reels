12) keyva-reel-12-india-250k-limit.mp4
------------------------------------------------------------
Buying in Dubai from India? Under the LRS, each resident can send up to USD 250,000 a financial year, all purposes combined. Co-owners on the title can each send their own. Past ₹10 lakh, your bank collects 20% TCS, which is credited against your income tax. NRIs: the scheme doesn't apply. Not tax advice. keyva.ae

13) keyva-reel-13-knowledge-fee.mp4
------------------------------------------------------------
Yes, Dubai has a "knowledge fee": AED 10, plus an AED 10 innovation fee, AED 250 for the title deed and AED 250 for the map. With a mortgage, some of them come twice. Those are the small ones. On top come the 4% transfer fee, trustee office registration, mortgage registration, bank valuation and agency commission. Keyva lists every fee and who sets it. keyva.ae

15) keyva-reel-15-off-plan-escrow.mp4
------------------------------------------------------------
Buying off-plan in Dubai? Your payments should go into the project's escrow account, not to the developer directly (Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007). Keyva's project check shows the status, escrow bank, developer licence and completion target on the register. Not legal advice. keyva.ae

16) keyva-reel-16-90-days-notice.mp4
------------------------------------------------------------
Landlord raising the rent? Check the date first. Any change to your contract, rent included, needs 90 days' written notice. Served late, an otherwise lawful increase doesn't bind you for that renewal. A guide, not a ruling. keyva.ae

------------------------------------------------------------
That car loan could shrink your Dubai mortgage. Keyva's mortgage calculator applies a 50% debt-burden cap: on AED 30,000 a month, that's AED 15,000 for all your loans. An AED 3,000 car loan leaves AED 12,000. Illustrative figures, not financial advice. keyva.ae

18) keyva-reel-18-deposit-not-law.mp4
------------------------------------------------------------
That 10% deposit cheque in Dubai? A market custom agreed between buyer and seller, not a law. What's official is Contract F, made by a registered broker in Dubai REST, with the price, the payment plan and who pays the transfer fee. Not legal advice. keyva.ae
